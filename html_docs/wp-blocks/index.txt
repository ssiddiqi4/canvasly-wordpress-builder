<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Canvasly</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Design a page on a canvas, then publish it the usual WordPress way. The editor has a unit library, desktop, tablet, and mobile views, and a design system for colors, type, and reusable parts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Free Canvasly Lite includes nested containers, CSS Grid, templates, and the core unit library.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Canvasly Pro adds theme parts, shop units, popups, forms logging, hosted payments, and an AI connection/MCP for AI assisted web page design.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pro loads only when Canvasly is active and at least version 0.12.73. WordPress 6.9 or newer and PHP 7.4 or newer are required.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">VidCellar</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Host and play videos on your own WordPress site. Lite stores videos on the server and plays them in a responsive player. Visitors can browse categories, search, rate, and watch without a separate video account.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Free VidCellar Lite includes the library, categories, thumbnails, trailers, ratings, and resumable uploads.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>VidCellar Pro adds Stripe and PayPal checkout, complimentary coupons, advertisement breaks, sales reports, and cloud storage.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pro requires VidCellar Lite. WordPress 6.2 or newer and PHP 7.4 or newer are required.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
</ul>
