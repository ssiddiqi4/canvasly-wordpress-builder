<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Canvasly questions</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className": "lede"} -->
<p class="lede">Answers below come from the plugin screens and readme. Items marked TODO are questions a site owner is likely to ask that the code does not answer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className": "meta"} -->
<p class="meta">Updated <time datetime="2026-09-22">September 22, 2026</time>. Covers Canvasly 0.12.76 and Canvasly Pro 0.6.3.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What do I need?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>WordPress 6.9 or later and PHP 7.4 or later. Canvasly Pro also needs Canvasly 0.12.73 or newer, and it shows a notice instead of loading when that is not true.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How do I edit a page?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open the page and choose Edit with Canvasly, or use the admin-bar link. Posts and Pages are editable immediately. Other public types are off until you enable them under Settings &#8594; General.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Where are the settings?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Canvasly &#8594; Settings covers post types, maps, reCAPTCHA, CSS, fonts, performance, tools, and experiments. Colors and breakpoints are under Design System. See the <a href="/canvasly-settings/">settings page</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The editor looks broken. What should I try?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Under Settings &#8594; Advanced, set Editor loader to Iframe. Under Settings &#8594; Tools, turn on Safe mode for your account. Safe mode loads the editor without other plugins and without the theme.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why is a unit missing from the library?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Units Manager can disable a type or hide it from your role. Nested Tabs, Accordion, and Toggle, Collection Loop, and Grid container can also be switched off under Settings &#8594; Features. Shop units are absent unless WooCommerce is active. Pro units are absent unless Canvasly Pro has started.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Can I reuse a section on another page?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Right-click a Container or Grid and choose Save as Template. Place it later with the Template unit, the shortcode <code>[canvasly_lite_template id="42"]</code>, the Canvasly Template block, or the Canvasly Template sidebar widget.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Will maintenance mode hide the site from me?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Roles you exclude still see the live site. Administrator is excluded by default. Coming soon keeps a normal response. Maintenance uses HTTP 503. Visitors see the template you pick, or a built-in page if you pick none.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Does Canvasly conflict with my theme or with Gutenberg?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The files do not name a plugin they refuse to run beside. On the normal WordPress editing screen, Canvasly does not load its full editor. The Template block and the Edit with Canvasly launcher still appear. You can also turn off Canvasly&#8217;s default colors and fonts so the theme keeps those.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How do I move the site to a new address?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use Canvasly &#8594; Settings &#8594; Tools &#8594; Replace URL. Run Dry run first. The replacement cannot be undone from that screen.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->

<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How do popups decide when to show?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Popup templates print in the footer, hidden, and open from load, scroll, click, exit, and inactivity triggers. Frequency uses a cookie from this site. A link action can open a popup.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Can I roll back an update?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Settings &#8594; Tools keeps previous plugin zips. The default is to keep 3, and you can keep from 1 to 10. The rollback list is on that tools screen when a zip has been stored.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Who can change colors for the whole site?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Design System and the editor&#8217;s Site Settings are for people with Full access in Role Manager. Content-only users can edit page content and do not see Style and Advanced. Administrators always have Full access.</p>
