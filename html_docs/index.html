Two WordPress plugins. Free to start. Pro when you need more.

WordPress tools for pages and video

Canvasly is a visual page builder. VidCellar is a video library that plays privately on your own site. Each one ships as a free plugin, and each has an optional Pro add-on that runs only while the free plugin is active.

Page builder

Canvasly

Design a page on a canvas, then publish it the usual WordPress way. The editor has a unit library, desktop, tablet, and mobile views, and a design system for colors, type, and reusable parts.

  • Free Canvasly Lite includes nested containers, CSS Grid, templates, and the core unit library.
  • Canvasly Pro adds theme parts, shop units, popups, forms logging, hosted payments, and an AI connection/MCP for AI assisted web page design.
  • Pro loads only when Canvasly is active and at least version 0.12.73. WordPress 6.9 or newer and PHP 7.4 or newer are required.

Video library

VidCellar

Host and play videos on your own WordPress site. Lite stores videos on the server and plays them in a responsive player. Visitors can browse categories, search, rate, and watch without a separate video account.

  • Free VidCellar Lite includes the library, categories, thumbnails, trailers, ratings, and resumable uploads.
  • VidCellar Pro adds Stripe and PayPal checkout, complimentary coupons, advertisement breaks, sales reports, and cloud storage.
  • Pro requires VidCellar Lite. WordPress 6.2 or newer and PHP 7.4 or newer are required.

What stays on your site

Canvasly documents and VidCellar video files live in WordPress. Neither free plugin replaces the other, and neither Pro plugin runs on its own. Choose a product from the menu, or open the Canvasly guide for install, settings, units, shortcodes, and AI connection.
